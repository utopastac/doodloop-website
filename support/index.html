How do we play together?

One person creates a lobby; others join from the browse list on the same Wi‑Fi or Personal Hotspot. You need at least three seats. You can add extra seats on one phone for pass-and-play.

Where is my art stored?

Avatars, in-progress rounds, and completed History stay on each phone. During a live game, drawings and guesses sync only to the other devices in that lobby over the local network. Uninstalling the app removes local History on that device.

Someone can’t find the lobby

Confirm Local Network permission is allowed for Doodloop, that everyone is on the same network (or hotspot), and that the host kept the app open. Brief disconnects get a short reconnect grace; if the host leaves for good, remaining phones may elect a new host so the room can continue.

Something looks wrong

Tell us your device model, iOS version, how many players / phones, and what you were doing when the issue happened. Screenshots help. Email archgrovehouse@gmail.com.
